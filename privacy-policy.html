Lapangly – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026 · App: Lapangly: Storage & Phone Care (com.lapangly.app) · Developer: Mohd Firdaus

Summary

Lapangly works entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, sell or share your personal data, files, photos, videos or list of apps. The app contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Information the app accesses on your device

  • Files in folders you choose. You select folders using Android's own folder picker. Lapangly reads file names, sizes, types and dates in those folders to show them to you, and may read file contents only to detect exact duplicates (by computing a checksum on your device). Files are deleted only when you select them and confirm. You can remove a folder at any time, which also removes the app's access.
  • Usage access (optional). If you switch on Usage access, Lapangly reads when your apps were last used and how much space they use, to show unused and large apps. This information is shown to you and is never sent anywhere.
  • Device status. Free storage, memory, battery level, health and temperature, time since restart, Android version and security patch date are read to display your phone checkup.

None of this information leaves your device. Lapangly has no servers and no user accounts.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Lapangly Pro" purchase is processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. The app receives only a purchase confirmation from Google Play so that Pro features can be unlocked, and stores an on/off flag on your device. Google's handling of payment data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Usage access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS) – optional, granted by you in Settings; used only as described above.
  • Request uninstall (REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES) – lets the app open Android's uninstall dialog for an app you choose. Android always asks you to confirm.
  • Billing – used by Google Play for the in-app purchase.

Lapangly does not request storage, camera, microphone, location, contacts or phone permissions.

Children

Lapangly is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Data deletion

Because we do not collect data, there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling the app removes all of its settings from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Lapangly – Dasar Privasi

Tarikh berkuat kuasa: 26 Oktober 2026 · Pembangun: Mohd Firdaus

Ringkasan

Lapangly berfungsi sepenuhnya di peranti anda. Kami tidak mengumpul, menyimpan, menjual atau berkongsi data peribadi, fail, foto, video atau senarai aplikasi anda. Tiada iklan, analitik atau penjejakan.

Maklumat yang diakses di peranti anda

  • Fail dalam folder yang anda pilih melalui pemilih folder Android. Lapangly membaca nama, saiz, jenis dan tarikh fail untuk dipaparkan kepada anda, dan mungkin membaca kandungan fail hanya untuk mengesan pendua (dengan checksum di peranti). Fail hanya dipadam apabila anda memilih dan mengesahkannya.
  • Akses penggunaan (pilihan) – untuk menunjukkan bila aplikasi terakhir digunakan dan saiznya. Tidak dihantar ke mana-mana.
  • Status peranti – storan, memori, bateri, masa sejak mula semula, versi Android dan tampung keselamatan untuk semakan telefon.

Pembelian

Pembelian "Lapangly Pro" diproses oleh Google Play. Kami tidak menerima butiran pembayaran anda; aplikasi hanya menerima pengesahan pembelian.
